Final Takeaways and Next Steps

Findings
  • Music has evolved over the last century. Acousticness and instrumentalness have decreased, while energy and danceability have increased—often reflecting historical events like the Great Depression and Great Recession.

  • Some musical features relate to popularity, others don’t. For example, acousticness and instrumentalness tend to be higher in less popular songs, suggesting a negative relationship. In contrast, features like liveness, speechiness, and valence show no strong pattern with popularity—points are widely scattered across all values.

Future studies
  • Use metrics like Billboard rankings or record sales to better reflect a song’s popularity at release, rather than Spotify’s current popularity score.

  • Build a regression model to predict a song’s popularity based on its features.

  • This model could be developed into an app or dashboard to help artists or producers estimate a song's potential success.
