This bar chart shows the popular musical quanlities of the most popular songs of each decade.

To view differnt decades, you can click below the chart to select a new one.



Visualization

Takeaways

This figure looks at the top 10% of songs from each decade, and the average level of the musical attributes across those songs. Users can click through the different decades at the bottom to compare different decades and see how the trends change over the course of a century. A key aspect of this is that it is only the most popular songs, allowing it to focus on not just what types of songs are available but what songs people enjoy from each era. This also means it represents what songs have had a lasting cultural impact to be what is popular today from that era.

Methodology

There are some pretty dramatic changes in some of the attributes’ levels shown here. Some features decrease in popularity over the decades, including acousticness, liveness, and instrumentalness. Acousticness is the most impressive change of these, decreasing from over 90% acoustic down to 20%. Attributes such as speechiness or liveness were never very popular and have always been low. For valence, its always around the middle of the score, demonstratintg the wide variety of songs that are popular. However, it is always leaning towards the happy/upbeat side, except for in the 1940s and 2010s. This indicates that the popular music produced in those decades was sadder. Lastly, danceability was always high but is getting higher, raising up to 0.7 in the current decade indicated an increase in demand for dancing music.
